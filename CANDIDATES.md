# Candidates — Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run

Six two-line candidates were drafted and compared pairwise. The winner:
traffic jam as a herd of metal elephants napping in the middle of the road.

Why it won: the elephant is concrete (a 10-year-old has seen one), and
"metal elephants deciding to nap in the middle of the road" is a surprising
combination. Line 1 is one sentence, one metaphor. Line 2 names two things
the metaphor maps (the slow shuffle, the way nothing can pass) and two it
leaves out (no car is sleepy — each is stuck because the road ahead is
already full), directly correcting the mistaken "decided to nap" idea
instead of praising the metaphor.

Runner-up: rain as the world's biggest shower. Not used: toaster as a "tiny
jail for bread" — another entrant had already posted the same idea in the
competition chat.

## The entry (259 characters, 2 lines)

A traffic jam is a herd of metal elephants that all decided to nap in the middle of the road.
The elephants fit the slow shuffle and the way nothing can pass, but they leave out that no car is sleepy — each one is stuck because the road ahead is already full.
